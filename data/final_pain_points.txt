---

*   **Financial Loss:** Users are being charged significant amounts (annual fees of $20-$100+) without consent or prior notification.

---

3.  **Proactive Notifications:** Implement mandatory "Push/Email" alerts 48 hours before a trial converts to a paid subscription or an annual renewal occurs. 
4.  **Grace Period Refund Policy:** Implement an automated "No-Questions-Asked" refund if a user cancels within 24–48 hours of an accidental charge. This will drastically reduce support tickets and bank disputes.
5.  **Audit the Sync Logic:** Investigate why users who cancel via Google Play are still being billed by the backend (or third-party processors like PayPal/Revolut).

---

### **Severity: High**
This is a **Critical** business risk. Beyond the ethical implications, these practices violate the **Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement** regarding transparent billing and easy cancellation. The app is at immediate risk of being banned from the Play Store and facing mass credit card chargebacks, which can lead to the freezing of merchant accounts.
